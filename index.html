Welcome to My GIS Portfolio

I am a graduate student pursuing a Master of Science in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) at the University of Cincinnati, with a background in Earth and Environmental Science.

My interests include environmental GIS, hydrologic analysis, remote sensing, spatial analysis, cartography, and geospatial data visualization.

This portfolio highlights selected projects demonstrating my experience with watershed analysis, terrain modeling, land-cover mapping, and urban spatial analysis.

Featured GIS Projects

Watershed Terrain & Precipitation Analysis

DEM-based terrain and hydrologic analysis of the San Marcos Basin, including slope, aspect, flow direction, ModelBuilder automation, zonal statistics, and precipitation interpolation.

Watershed Database & Hydrologic Mapping

Development of an integrated watershed GIS database containing land cover, catchments, stream networks, basin boundaries, and USGS monitoring stations.

Urban Land Cover Analysis

Comparative land-cover analysis examining urban and environmental patterns in Baton Rouge, Buffalo, and Omaha.

Cincinnati Park Accessibility

Urban GIS analysis using buffers to examine walkable access to parks and green spaces in Cincinnati.
